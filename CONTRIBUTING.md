# Contributing to florn

The routine, the rules and the crate map are in the book: `docs/src/contributing.md` and `docs/src/architecture.md` (built by `mdbook build docs`). Decisions are RFCs under `rfcs/`; the handoff package of each phase under `rfcs/handoffs/` says how the work was planned and checked. The roles and the authorisations are in `docs/src/contributing.md`.
